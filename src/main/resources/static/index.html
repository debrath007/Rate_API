Account rates

Shows the effective rate per rate type — the lower of the normal rate and any override.

Adjust all rates

Applies a percentage change to every account and rate type. Positive increases, negative decreases. A backup is taken automatically before anything is written.

Backups

Every adjustment snapshots the workbook first. Restoring overwrites the live file with the newest snapshot.

Rate policy compliance

Checks the workbook against company policy: SCRA accounts must sit at exactly 6.00% regardless of rate deployments, no card member may be charged above 29.99% APR, and where both a normal and an override rate exist the lower must be charged. The SME review adds an expert reading of the findings — it calls the Claude CLI, takes around 30 seconds, and consumes API tokens.

Run tests

Runs the project's Gradle test task and reports each test case. Tests use their own throwaway copy of the workbook, so this never touches your real data.
